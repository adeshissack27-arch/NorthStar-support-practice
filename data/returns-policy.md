# Northstar Returns and Refund Policy

## Return Period

Customers can request a return within 30 days of receiving their product.

## Return Condition

The product should be unused and in acceptable condition.

## Refunds

A refund is processed after the returned product has been inspected and approved.

## Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds normally take 5–7 business days to appear.

## How to Request a Return

Customers should provide their order ID and explain the reason for the return.

## Important

If a customer has a problem that is not covered by this policy, the request should be escalated to a human support agent.
